# Pawsport: roadmap

What's done, what's next. Read this first when picking the work up again.

## Done
- Pixel cats on the desktop: 24 coats, cards with genes, care, wardrobe, home and garden
- The world: 204 places, flights that take time, parks with their own animals, city markets and souvenirs
- 💡 A "Did you know?" fact for every place, on its street and in the passport album

- Sky events: full moon, UFO, meteor shower, rainbow, northern lights

- Save file (ready for Steam Cloud), Steam achievements bridge, `npm run dist:steam`
- Trailers: 30 s landscape (Steam) and vertical (Shorts / Reels), with original music
- Name: **Pawsport**; licence: all rights reserved
